About me

A short, honest line about who you are and what you do — the kind of sentence you'd say if someone asked "so what do you do?" at a party.

Use this paragraph to talk through your background: how you got into your field, what you studied or taught yourself, and the kind of problems you like solving. Keep it in your own voice rather than resume language — this is the page where personality is allowed to show.

A second paragraph is a good place for what you're working on now, what you're curious about, or what you're looking for next — a role, a collaboration, a project. End with something that invites people to reach out.
